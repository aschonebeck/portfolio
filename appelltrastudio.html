Designing a digital platform for an independent woodworking studio rooted in craftsmanship




The goal was to translate the tactile quality of handmade woodwork into a web experience as refined as the studio's output.

Working closely with the founder, I designed a responsive site that lets the pieces speak — generous spacing, detailed imagery, and navigation kept simple and clear.

  • Visual identity translated into layout - spacing, type and image treatment that echo the studio's material sensibility
  • Information architecture shaped around how people browse and discover objects, not just navigate pages
  • Responsive front-end built with performance and accessibility as part of the craft
  • Close collaboration with the founder throughout, from direction to launch
