Forming a Design Studio

YEAR: 2020-23
ORG: Decent People
ROLE: Co-founder & UX Designer

UX Designer
Stockholm, SWE

I was part of co-founding a design studio focused on human-centered and co-creative design practice. We supported other organisations in their creative processes and challenges by combining design sprints, workshops, UX research, web- and UI design.

OFFERING

Our focus was on understanding real needs, testing early, and shaping ideas that made sense in the world they’d live in. Based on our clients needs and our observations of what was required in each process, we delivered:

→ Design Sprints
Structured frameworks to align stakeholders, prototype ideas and test solutions early.

→ Workshops and Facilitation
Customised sessions to unlock creativity, build shared understanding, and strengthen team collaboration.

→ UX Projects
Design work for digital products and services, spanning user research, idea generation, prototyping,

→ Lectures
Hands-on sessions for organisations and schools.
