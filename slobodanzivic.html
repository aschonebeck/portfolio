A digital space for an artist through the principle of subtraction






## The brief was to create an online space that would present Slobodan Zivic's work without imposing unnecessary visual or structural layers. I approached the website through design by subtraction, reducing the interface to essential HTML elements, typography, imagery and navigation — allowing the artist's work and the structure of the site itself to shape the experience.


  • ** Design by subtraction — removed decorative UI and conventional portfolio patterns in favour of only what is essential
  • ** Content as interface — typography, imagery and HTML structure carry both the visual language and navigation
  • ** Archive as experience — exhibitions, curatorial work and other material are presented as an evolving index rather than separated into conventional pages
  • ** Designed around the practice — the reduced system reflects Zivic's own approach to stripping away irrelevant information and questioning conventional structures
  • ** Accessible foundations — relied on semantic HTML, straightforward navigation and a reduced information hierarchy to create a robust foundation for different ways of accessing the site

Rather than treating minimalism as an aesthetic alone, the reduction became a way of thinking about usability and accessibility. Removing unnecessary interface elements reduces visual and cognitive noise, while a simple structure and reliance on fundamental web elements create a more predictable and intuitive experience.
