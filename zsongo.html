Web design and development for a culture-driven events platform




Though rooted in Madrid, Zsongo hosts events around the globe and is all about celebrating diversity, culture, and human connection.

The goal was to create a site that feels as dynamic and alive as the world it represents. Every element was designed to reflect its essence, making the digital space an extension of Zsongo's creative spirit.

  • Typography, spacing & composition built around the vibrancy of the culture-driven events
  • Animation and motion used to carry the energy of an expressive brand without tipping into noise, balancing aliveness with usability
  • Responsive layout decisions made ensuring the website holds across screen sizes without breaking the experience
  • From concept to launch in 5 weeks
  • Ongoing ownership of the site, keeping content current as the platform grows
