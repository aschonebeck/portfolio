Amanda Schönebeck

UX Designer with a background in computer science, working across interaction design, systems and writing. Over the past several years I have worked within studios, institutions and independent practice, with a focus on how digital and physical experiences shape the way people move through everyday life.

My work sits at the intersection of structure and language: how things are organised, how they communicate, and what gets lost in the gap between the two. Thinking about flow, context, and the small decisions that makes something feels considered or not.

Writing runs alongside my design work, as a way of testing ideas, and exploring what good design is actually trying to do.
