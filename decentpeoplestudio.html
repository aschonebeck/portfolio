UX DESIGN, 2020-23

Decent People — Design Studio

Role: Co-Founder / Designer

I was part of co-founding a design studio focused on human-centered and co-creative design practice. We supported other organisations in their creative processes and challenges by combining design sprints, workshops, UX research, UI Design, and other tailored methods.

OFFERING

Our focus was on understanding real needs, testing early, and shaping ideas that made sense in the world they’d live in. Based on our clients’ needs and our observations of what was required in each process, we delivered:

→ Design Sprints — structured frameworks to align stakeholders, prototype ideas and test solutions early.

→ Workshops and Facilitation — customised sessions to unlock creativity, build shared understanding, and strengthen team collaboration.

→ UX Projects — design work for digital platforms and products, spanning user research, idea generation, prototyping,

→ Lectures — hands-on sessions for organisations and schools.
